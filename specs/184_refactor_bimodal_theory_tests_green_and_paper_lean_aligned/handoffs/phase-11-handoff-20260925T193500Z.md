## Key Decisions Made

- **`truth_value_at` returns `None`, never `False`, for a missing certificate or an
  out-of-range lasso index.** Matches `set_colors`'s own "neither true nor false" branch
  (prints a warning rather than silently treating an unevaluated proposition as false) and D8's
  never-claim-validity spirit extended to the proposition level: "no certificate" must not look
  like "false everywhere."
- **World state = lasso index, not a bitvector repr.** The certificate encoding's carrier is
  `{0,...,k} x Z` directly (`docs/ADEQUACY.md` section 3) -- there is no separate finite "state"
  layer a `(lasso, position)` point decomposes into, unlike the retired encoding's
  `BitVec[N]`-valued world-state array. `_find_proposition_at`'s `truth_set`/`false_set` are
  therefore sets of plain `int` lasso indices; `pretty_set_print` handles them fine (it
  `str()`s and sorts elements generically).
- **No recursion through operator classes anywhere in this file.** Every truth-value query is a
  single label-membership test regardless of whether `sentence` is atomic or compound. This
  depends on `finalize_certificate`'s local-coherence constraints (Phase 9) already having tied
  every compound's bit to its constituents' at extraction time -- verified directly by the
  negation/box/until compound tests, not merely assumed.

## What NOT to Try

- Do not add back a `contingent`/`disjoint`-style constraint path "just in case" -- those
  settings are gone (D4, Phase 9) and atoms are unconstrained by design, not by omission.
- Do not make `truth_value_at` recurse through `sentence.operator` for compound sentences --
  the whole point of the certificate design is that label membership already IS the recursive
  answer, once local coherence has been asserted. Re-adding recursion here would risk disagreeing
  with what the Z3 constraints already enforce.
- Do not treat "no certificate" as `False` anywhere in this file or its callers -- always `None`
  (D8, extended to the proposition level as recorded above).
