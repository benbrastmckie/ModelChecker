### Root-cause correction (Entry 2), verified declaration-by-declaration

The dispatch's own mid-text correction states the collapse has no temporal content — it is a reflexivity-derived invariance axiom, and the real defect is that one relation (`share`) is asked to do two incompatible jobs (an equivalence for the stability modal; a non-symmetric, non-transitive one-step relation for succession). Verified fully against the live tree:

- **`snce_share_congr` is literally two readings of one clause via reflexivity**, `Incompleteness.lean:108-113`:
  ```
  theorem snce_share_congr ... :=
    ((hloc i t).2.2.2.2 j hij g e hc).trans
      ((hloc j t).2.2.2.2 j (S.share_refl t j) g e hc).symm
  ```
  Its own docstring states this precisely: "Both readings are of the *one* clause: at `i` with `k := j`, and at `j` with `k := j` by reflexivity of `share`. No other condition is used, and no bound appears." This is the "instantiate the clause twice, chain the two biconditionals" mechanism from the correction, confirmed at the declaration.
- **`share` is a landed equivalence relation**: `SharingWitnessFamily.share_refl` / `share_symm` / `share_trans` all exist (`WitnessFamily/Sharing/Basic.lean:166-174`), matching "the sharing relation is literally an equality of representatives, hence an equivalence."
- **`Thread.step` is *defined* via `share`**, not merely related to it: `structure Thread (K : SharingSkeleton) where ... step : ∀ u : ℤ, K.share (u + 1) (idx u) (idx (u + 1))` (`Sharing/Skeleton.lean:301-305`). Because `share` is symmetric and transitive, the one-step succession relation built directly from it inherits both properties — confirming "succession inherits symmetry and transitivity" exactly as the correction states, with no interpretive leap required.
- **The until-side mirror is `plusSnce_thread_step`** (misnamed in the dispatch's phrasing as the "escape" clause; it is in fact the confirmation that no escape exists), `PlusWitnessFamily/Fulfil.lean:344-350`, which reads the `snce` clause at `t-1` via `plusThread_share_pred` — itself derived from `Thread.step (t-1)` read backwards via `share_symm`. Task 696's own round-2 report states this explicitly: "The current `snce` clause is therefore already the exact mirror of the `untl` clause relative to `Thread.step`; the asymmetry the task description attributes to 'own time vs `t+1`' is not the mechanism... Both clauses quantify over the one-step reach of a position, and both collapse."
- **The until-side gate family exists and is machine-checked, but is not yet landed in the library.** `untl_shift_share_congr`, `not_plusCertifies_stabUntl`, `not_plusValidZTime_stabUntl` are found only in `specs/696_stability_modal_substrate_design/probes/01_untl_shift_congr_probe.lean` and `probes/02_untl_target_nonvalid_probe.lean` (both `lake env lean ...` exit 0, per task 696's own reports) — a `grep` over `FormalSystem/` for these three names returns nothing. Task 696's own implementation plan step 0 is to land these from the probes into `Incompleteness.lean` proper, which has not yet happened (696 is still `researched`, not `implementing`/`completed`). **The rewritten Entry 2 should describe this gate family as "machine-checked in an archived upstream probe, not yet landed in the library," not as an already-permanent theorem.**
- **696's own recommended redesign confirms the "two jobs, one relation" diagnosis is the design driver**: its recommended `SharingSkeleton` addition is a *new*, independent `trans` relation (`transBack transMid transFwd`, decoded with a reflexive-only default, `trans u i j := transRaw u i j = true ∧ share (u+1) i j`) specifically to carry one-step succession separately from `share`, which remains the equivalence the stability modal quantifies over. This is precisely "separate the branching substrate's two roles" rather than repairing `share` itself.
