## Key Decisions Made

- Z3 clauses iterate concrete states in Python (`z3.And([...])` over `all_states`) with the concrete-world truth subterm memoized per semantics instance, instead of `utils.ForAll` substitution, so the truth clause is built once per world.
- The predicate encoding uses `Not(cf_true(w))` for falsity; licensed by the complementarity test (recursive truth and falsity clauses are complementary at every world on every solved model).
- `truth_predicate` is only sound before solving (its defining constraints are appended to `semantics.frame_constraints` on creation); post-solve evaluations in tests pass `DIRECT` explicitly.
- Python side computes `T(w)` from the antecedent's proposition verifiers, `is_alternative` evaluated in the model, and the recursive `true_at`/`false_at` of the consequent -- the same route the status quo takes, independent of the candidate's Z3 clause.
- Cost at N=4 (direct encoding): nested antecedent solves in about 1.2 s for W/L; no encoding change needed yet. Phase 4 measures this properly.
