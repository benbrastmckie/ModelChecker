- **Deleted three CI contract test files outright rather than rewriting them**, after confirming
  each one's entire subject matter (the `development` marker's application, or bimodal's
  solve-cost decoupling) evaporated with the mechanism it tested -- there was nothing left to
  assert. Contrast with `test_run_tests_markers.py`/`test_unstable_deselection_wiring.py`, which

- **Left `.github/workflows/unstable-watch.yml`'s `watch_development` step and
  `.github/scripts/unstable_watch_classify.py`'s `DEV_STATUS` classification path untouched.**
  With no test carrying `development` any more, that step now always collects zero items (a
  harmless, already-tolerated no-op). Retiring it is not required for bimodal's own exit from
  development and was left for whoever next revisits that observer, not folded into this
  retirement.
- **Left `docs/ADEQUACY.md` and its one stale sentence untouched** (already recorded in Phase 22's
  own handoff) -- unrelated to this phase, restated here only because Phase 24 should not
  rediscover it as new.
- **Rewrote TESTING_GUIDE.md section 8.14 as a compact retirement record (~100 lines) rather than
  editing the prior ~310-line procedure in place** -- most of that text described a mechanism that
  no longer exists (the theory-wide blanket, its containment tests, its exact "ten invocations"
  narrative); preserving it edited-in-place would have left far more dead prose than a clean
  retirement record.
- **Did not attempt to fix `run-oracle-suite.sh`'s pass-2 behavior directly** -- confirmed it now
  passes as an emergent consequence of the marker's removal, not something this phase needed to
  engineer.
