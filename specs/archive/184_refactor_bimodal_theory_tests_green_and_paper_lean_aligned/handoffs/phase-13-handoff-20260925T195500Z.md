## Key Decisions Made

- **A0's two axioms are posed as the sole conclusion of an empty-premise search, not as a
  premise.** An earlier draft of this test put them in `premises`, which only asks "is this
  formula satisfiable" (nearly always yes) -- not "does a Z-time countermodel to its *validity*
  exist" (A0's actual question). Caught by the test itself failing loudly (`z3_model_status is
  True` when `False` was expected) rather than by inspection -- worth restating for whoever next
  writes a similar minimum-frame-class deciding test: **pose the axiom as the conclusion of an
  empty-premise search**, not as a premise.
- **`A \Until B`, in ModelChecker's own infix surface syntax, means "B holds until A happens"
  (event=A, guard=B) -- the reverse of a naive left-to-right reading.** Confirmed directly from
  `UntilOperator.true_at(self, event_arg, guard_arg, eval_point)`'s parameter order matched
  against how `sentence.arguments` populates positional call arguments. This bit two of this
  phase's own A0 formulas before being caught by the standing test itself reporting the wrong
  verdict -- see D2's own note in the plan; Phase 16's audit of the BX examples' `\Until`/`\Since`
  argument order needs exactly this same care.
- **Unary prefix operators (`\future`, `\Future`, `\neg`, ...) chain directly onto their argument
  without wrapping parens** (`\future \Future A`, not `\future (\Future A)`) -- confirmed against
  `examples.py`'s own existing convention (`'\\Future \\past A'`) and its explicit comment on the
  point. Wrapping an already-unary chain in extra parens is a parser error ("Operator missing
  after operand"), not merely redundant.
- **No fault-injection test for the `ModelConstructionError` raise path.** Constructing a
  genuinely wrong Z3 model to trigger it would need monkeypatching `witness_constraints.py` or
  `extract_certificate` to actually misbehave -- the mechanism it calls (`certificate.recheck`)
  already has exhaustive direct unit coverage from Phase 4, so this was judged low marginal value
  against the effort of building a realistic fault injection. Flag if a future phase finds this
  path is untested in a way that matters.

## What NOT to Try

- Do not pose a minimum-frame-class "no certificate should ever exist" axiom as a `premises`
  entry -- always as the sole `conclusions` entry with empty `premises` (see "Key Decisions Made"
  above).
- Do not assume `A \Until B` means "A holds until B" by naive reading -- it is event-first,
  meaning "B holds until A happens." Re-derive from `UntilOperator.true_at`'s signature if in
  doubt, do not guess from the infix spelling.
- Do not try to make operators.py's print methods work "for free" by leaving them as-is -- they
  still assume the retired `world_history`/`get_world_history` surface and will need Phase 14's
  full rewrite regardless of how correct `semantic/model.py`'s own printing is.
- Do not chase every one of the 149 currently-failing tests individually before Phase 14/18 --
  the great majority trace to callers of deleted attributes/methods (`get_world_array`,
  `world_histories`, `M`, `all_times`, quantifier-based operator internals) that Phases 14/18
  are the designated fix points for, not this phase's own regression.
