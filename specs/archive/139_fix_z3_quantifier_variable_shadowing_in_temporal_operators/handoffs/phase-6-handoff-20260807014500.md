    `test_mixed_and_all_future_neg`, plus corroborating context) passed cleanly when re-run in

    the identical PRE_FIX_SHA code passed consistently in 4-6s. Confirmed via a direct 60s-budget
    run that the countermodel is still genuinely found (~24s) -- a solve-time regression, not a
    correctness defect, with the same root cause Phase 2 already documented for `Box(p)->Box(p)`
    (loss of a Z3 term-identity-based simplification shortcut once bound variables are no longer
    accidentally shared). **Fixed forward**: widened both tests' local `max_time` from 15 to 30
    (not a pinned artifact); both confirmed green after the fix (24.66s combined).
  - 1 (`test_spot_check_individual_countermodels`) investigated in depth: post-fix,
    `F9_until_implies_event` unexpectedly got a countermodel instead of the expected `None`
    (valid). Verified against PRE_FIX_SHA that **all four** candidate formulas (F4/F7/F9/F10)
    were *already timing out* pre-fix (never a confident "valid" verdict) -- the test's own
    pre-existing docstring already anticipated this. Concluded this is "previously undecided ->
    now decided," not a regression, but the mathematical correctness of the new decided answer
    was not independently re-derived here (recorded as a follow-up, not asserted either way).
  - 2 (`test_all_sat_task_relation_ternary`, `test_all_sat_results_have_complete_output`) were
    **not** independently re-verified, per an explicit time-boxing direction received mid-phase
    to close out Phase 6 on the evidence already in hand rather than launch further confirmation
