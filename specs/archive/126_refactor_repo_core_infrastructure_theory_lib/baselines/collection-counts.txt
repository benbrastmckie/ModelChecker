=== NOTE ===
A bare root-level `pytest --collect-only -q` (no cd into code/, no explicit path scoping) does
NOT apply code/pyproject.toml's testpaths and instead walks the whole repo including
code/boneyard/, producing a misleading inflated count with collection errors. Phase 5 of this
plan deletes code/boneyard/, which is the correct fix for that trap (see task-122's
collection-counts.txt for the historical reproduction of the same issue).
