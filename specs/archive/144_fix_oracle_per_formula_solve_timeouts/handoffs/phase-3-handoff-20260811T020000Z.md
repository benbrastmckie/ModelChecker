# Phase 3 Handoff: Shift-Dimension Grounding (Rejected Candidate)

- **Task**: 144 - fix_oracle_per_formula_solve_timeouts
- **Phase**: 3 of 5

## What Was Tried

`depth_bounded_skolem_abundance_constraint`'s joint `ForAll([source_world, shift_amount], ...)` was
replaced with `2*max_shift` separate `ForAll([source_world], ...)` constraints, one per concrete
shift value `k` in `{-max_shift..max_shift}\{0}` (exactly `{-1, 1}` for all three Phase 1 target
formulas), substituting `k` as a `z3.IntVal` everywhere `shift_amount` previously appeared
(including inside `shift_of_bounded(source_world, k)` and
`matching_states_when_shifted_var(source_world, k, ...)`). This is a sound, logically-equivalent
finite-domain elimination (the shift range is a construction-time-known Python `int` range, never a
solver-time unknown), and deliberately left the world/target dimension fully quantified/Skolemized
-- unlike established dead end 1, which grounded the world dimension and regressed both SAT and
UNSAT in task 98. A `DEPTH_BOUNDED_ABUNDANCE_UNROLL_THRESHOLD = 8` fallback guarded against
unrolling an unboundedly large shift range for hypothetical deeper formulas.

## Why It Was Rejected

A paired 5-seed/7-run measurement round against the Phase 1 baseline showed a genuine cost
**regression**, not a trigger-suppression correctness issue like Phase 2's dead ends:

| Formula | Metric | Baseline | Phase 3 (unrolled) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `and_box_next` | median(wall) | 46.54s | 60.25s | worse (+29%) |
| `and_box_next` | seed 0 behavior | reliable ~45s solve | hard 60s timeout, all 3 runs | worse |
| `and_all_future_neg` | median(rlimit) | 61,308,987 | 124,635,923 | worse (+103%) |
| `and_all_future_neg` | median(wall) | 18.61s | 38.65s | worse (+108%) |
| `all_sat_task_relation_ternary` | median(rlimit) | 239,039,225 | 13,863,144 | better (-94%) |
| `all_sat_task_relation_ternary` | max(rlimit) | 339,149,009 | 444,437,378 | **worse** (fails rule B) |

Two of three targets regressed outright on both metrics; the third improved its median sharply but
violated acceptance rule B (worst case must not worsen) on its own. The most plausible mechanism:
splitting one joint two-variable quantifier into `2*max_shift` independent top-level quantifiers
increases the total number of quantifiers Z3's MBQI layer must schedule across the whole
frame-constraint set, and this scheduling overhead outweighed the benefit of each individual
quantifier being simpler. This is a genuinely new empirical finding, not previously documented
among the six established dead ends -- it does not overlap with dead end 1 (which is about grounding
the *world* dimension into `O(worlds^2)` ground disjunctions, a categorically different and much
larger transformation).

Per the plan's binding acceptance rules, a regressive candidate ("median cost up, or `max(rlimit)`
up") is reverted immediately, which was done. The encoding is now byte-behavior-identical to the
Phase 1 baseline (confirmed: seed-0 `and_box_next` rlimit=130120807, exact match). The only residual
diff versus the pre-task-144 codebase is the dead-end-10 comment plus a harmless return-type change
(`depth_bounded_skolem_abundance_constraint` now always returns a single-element list rather than a
bare Z3 expression, with the caller updated to match -- this has zero solve-cost or semantic effect,
verified by the exact rlimit match above).

## New Dead End (continuing the numbering from Phase 2's 7, 8, 9)

10. **Unrolling `depth_bounded_skolem_abundance_constraint`'s `shift_amount` dimension into
    `2*max_shift` separate single-variable `ForAll`s** (Task 144): sound, logically-equivalent
    finite-domain elimination that leaves the world/target dimension fully quantified (NOT the same
    transformation as dead end 1). Measured regressive on 2 of 3 Phase 1 target formulas (worse
    median rlimit and wall-clock) and rule-B-violating on the third (worse `max(rlimit)` despite a
    much-improved median) -- likely because splitting one joint quantifier into many independent
    top-level quantifiers increases Z3 MBQI scheduling overhead across the whole frame-constraint
    set, outweighing each individual quantifier's simplicity. Reverted; recorded inline at
    `depth_bounded_skolem_abundance_constraint` in `core.py`.
