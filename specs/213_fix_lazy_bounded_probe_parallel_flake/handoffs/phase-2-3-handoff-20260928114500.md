  local-variable-binding form (`code = "..."` then referenced by name, even nested one level

  and bound-assert detectors against the union of the function's own AST and every embedded tree.

  `test_scan_finds_the_known_marked_inventory`) now route through the deep helpers, closing the
  drift risk the plan flagged.
- Two new self-tests: `test_embedded_subprocess_source_is_flagged_when_unmarked` (positive) and
  `test_embedded_subprocess_source_respects_marker_suppression` (negative, `xdist_serial`-marked
  variant only — the `performance` variant was left as the plan's declared optional extra).
- Module docstring extended with the embedded-source-string route, its declared limits, and the
  reasoning for excluding `os.system` from the recognized call-target set.
