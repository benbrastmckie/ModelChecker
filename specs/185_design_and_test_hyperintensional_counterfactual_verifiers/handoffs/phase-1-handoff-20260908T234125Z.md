## Key Decisions Made

- The audit example is `\Box (A \boxright C)` at N=3 (not CF_CM_1): the box forces the counterfactual true at every world and the contingent letter forces at least two worlds, so the Z3-side `{w}` and the Python-side "all true worlds" are guaranteed to differ.
- `harness.solve()` repeats `run_test`'s pipeline and interprets premises and conclusions so sentence objects carry propositions (needed for Python-side/Z3-side cross-validation).
- Suffixed operator names (`\boxrightI` etc.) tokenize: the parser splits on whitespace after padding parentheses (`syntactic/sentence.py:141`), so a registered `\boxrightK` is a single token. Only registration is needed.
- `ModelConstraints.__init__` (`models/constraints.py:80-101`) reads `semantics.frame_constraints` by reference and concatenates after premise/conclusion construction, so clauses that append definitional constraints during construction are collected.
